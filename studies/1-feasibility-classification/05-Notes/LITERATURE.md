# Literature and novelty boundaries

Primary sources considered in the strategy review on 7 October 2026. This is a focused starting map, not a systematic novelty review.

| Source | Established territory | Implication for this pilot |
|---|---|---|
| [Kashtan and Alon 2005](https://www.weizmann.ac.il/mcb/alon/sites/mcb.UriAlon/files/spontaneousevolutionofmodularityandnetworkmotifs_0.pdf) | Modularly varying goals can produce modular networks through evolution. | Changing goals creates modularity is not a new claim. |
| [Yang et al. 2019](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41593-018-0310-2) | Task representations, functional clusters and sequential training in recurrent networks. | Compare computational specialization with preservation under learning. |
| [Why Transformers Succeed and Fail at Compositional Generalization](https://openreview.net/pdf?id=ADeeoMY4Dn) | Composition equivalence and module coverage can explain apparent generalization. | Equalize task coverage and distinguish new argument combinations from new compositions. |
| [Circuit explained 2026](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0340088) | Causal analysis of compositional generalization in a small transformer. | Finding a compositional circuit alone is insufficient differentiation. |
| [Is Grokking Worthwhile 2026 preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.09049) | Behavioral generalization and circuit formation can dissociate; transfer to new facts is limited in the studied systems. | A dissociation alone is not new; test a controlled cause and selective-update mechanism. |

Before scientific freeze, check continual-learning and curriculum papers specifically for matched-data ordering experiments with causal tests of selective plasticity. The proposed distinction between modular computation and modular learning remains a candidate contribution. AI-05 supplies local small-network and interchange experience; it does not validate this new task or its training regime.
